# Official Exam Materials

This folder stores official Czech A2 permanent-residence exam materials that we are permitted to store and use.

## Required materials

1. Official 2026 candidate handbook
2. Official 2026 model test
3. Official preparation materials, where downloadable and permitted
4. Notes extracted from the official materials

## Source of truth

Official website:
https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/en/

## Import checklist

- [ ] Download the official 2026 handbook PDF.
- [ ] Download the official 2026 model-test PDF.
- [ ] Upload the PDFs to this folder, if permitted.
- [ ] Check that the files open correctly.
- [ ] Keep exam requirements and source notes up to date.

Do not upload materials unless their license or terms allow it. Keep external links for materials that cannot be stored or redistributed.
