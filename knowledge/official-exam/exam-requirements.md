# Czech A2 Coach — Official Exam Requirements

## Source

Official 2026 Model Test: Czech A2 examination for permanent residence in the Czech Republic.

Official website: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/en/

Official preparation materials: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/a2/en/priprava-na-zkousku/

This document summarizes the 2026 model-test materials. Check the official website for updates before relying on these requirements.

## 1. Exam Structure

The examination assesses four skills:

- Reading
- Writing
- Listening
- Speaking

The written part includes Reading and Writing, followed by Listening. Speaking is assessed separately.

| Section | Approximate duration | Maximum points |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reading | 40 minutes | 25 |
| Writing | 25 minutes | 20 |
| Listening | About 40 minutes | 25 |
| Speaking | 15 minutes | 40 |

## 2. Passing Requirements in the 2026 Model Test

According to the 2026 model-test materials:

- Reading, Writing, and Listening together require at least 42 points out of 70.
- Speaking requires at least 24 points out of 40.
- The written and speaking requirements must both be met.

These are model-test requirements documented in the source. Verify the current official rules before treating them as definitive for a real examination.

## 3. Reading

The model test contains five task types:

1. Match pictures with practical information.
2. Match short texts to people.
3. Read texts and select the correct answer.
4. Complete texts by selecting missing information.
5. Complete practical information.

Maximum score: 25 points.

The tutor should practice:
- Identifying key information
- Understanding everyday notices and messages
- Matching information to a person's needs
- Reading short practical texts
- Selecting answers based on evidence in the text

## 4. Writing

The model test contains two tasks.

### Task 1 — Form or Short Answers

- Answer three questions.
- Write a complete sentence for each answer.
- Each answer must contain at least 10 words, according to the model-test instructions.
- Maximum score: 8 points.

### Task 2 — Email

- Write an email of at least 35 words.
- Include a request or instruction as required by the task.
- Address the information requested by the pictures or prompts.
- Maximum score: 12 points.
- The model-test materials state that an email shorter than 35 words receives zero points.

The tutor should check task completion, relevance, sentence formation, grammar, vocabulary, and word count.

## 5. Listening

The model test contains five task types:

1. Listen to dialogues and select answers.
2. Listen to short texts and select answers.
3. Match spoken texts to options.
4. Match dialogues to pictures.
5. Listen to a voice message and write the requested information.

Some recordings are played twice, as specified in the task instructions.

Maximum score: 25 points.

Practice should include:
- Names and personal information
- Numbers, dates, and times
- Prices and locations
- Directions and everyday arrangements
- What someone did, wants, or will do

## 6. Speaking

The model test contains four tasks.

### Task 1 — Answer Questions
- Eight questions across two everyday topics.
- Maximum score: 8 points.

### Task 2 — Ask for Information
- Two dialogues.
- Four questions in each dialogue.
- Maximum score: 12 points.

### Task 3 — Describe Pictures
- Describe a sequence of four pictures.
- Use the past tense to tell what happened.
- Maximum score: 10 points.

### Task 4 — Choose an Offer
- Discuss three offers or options.
- Ask or respond to relevant questions.
- Explain the preferred choice.
- Practice informal address (tykání) as specified by the task.
- Maximum score: 10 points.

The speaking section is worth 40 points, including the pronunciation component described in the model-test materials.

## 7. Important Communication Skills

The tutor should train students to:

- Answer questions in complete sentences.
- Respond to the specific question word, such as Kde, Kdy, Proč, Kam, or Jak dlouho.
- Use appropriate present, past, and future forms.
- Ask questions correctly.
- Request repetition or clarification when necessary.
- Distinguish formal address (vykání) from informal address (tykání).
- Explain choices and give simple reasons.
- Communicate clearly in everyday situations.

## 8. AI Tutor Boundaries

The tutor must:

- Never claim to be the official examiner.
- Never guarantee that a student will pass.
- Never invent official requirements or scoring rules.
- Clearly label practice scores as estimates, not official results.
- Create original practice exercises rather than falsely presenting generated questions as official questions.
- Explain uncertainty and direct learners to official sources for current exam rules.
