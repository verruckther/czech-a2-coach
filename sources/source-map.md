# Czech A2 Coach — Textbook & Teaching Source Map

## Purpose

This document defines which sources the AI tutor should use, what each source is for, and which sources have authority over exam requirements.

## Priority 1 — Official Exam Sources

**Purpose:** Establish the exam format, requirements, timing, scoring, and task types.

### Official permanent-residence Czech exam website
- URL: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/en/
- Use for: Official exam information and updates.
- Authority: Primary source for official exam requirements.

### Official 2026 handbook
- URL: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Prirucka_pro_uchazece_2026.pdf
- Use for: Candidate guidance and exam preparation information.
- Authority: Official exam source.

### Official new-format model test
- URL: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/a2/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2025/10/Modelovy-test-A2-novy-format-od-11.-dubna-2026.pdf
- Use for: Understanding official task formats and creating original, similar practice exercises.
- Authority: Official reference for the model-test format.

### Official preparation materials
- URL: https://cestina-pro-cizince.cz/trvaly-pobyt/a2/en/priprava-na-zkousku/
- Use for: Preparation guidance and links to official materials.

**Rules**
1. Official sources take priority for exam requirements and scoring.
2. Check for newer official information before making claims about current requirements.
3. Never invent exam rules, task formats, or passing criteria.
4. Do not present AI practice results as official exam results.
5. Do not reproduce protected exam content without permission.

## Priority 2 — Czech Textbooks and Teaching Materials

**Purpose:** Teach Czech grammar, vocabulary, sentence formation, and practical communication.

### Mozaika — Useful online Czech-learning resources
- URL: https://www.mozaika.eu/cestina-pro-cizince-online-uzitecne-odkazy-pro-studenty-ceskeho-jazyka/
- Type: Curated directory of Czech-learning resources.
- Use for: Discovering textbooks, workbooks, grammar references, vocabulary tools, exercises, and audio.
- Priority: High as a resource directory.
- Important: Evaluate individual resources before treating them as primary teaching materials.

### Selected textbooks and courses
Potential resources to evaluate:
- Cvičebnice pro azylanty a cizince A2
- Česky systematicky
- Česky krok za krokem
- Čeština expres

For each resource, record:
- Exact title and author
- Level
- Available format
- Topics and skills covered
- Access link
- Whether use or storage is permitted
- Recommended role in the tutor

Do not assume a resource is freely reusable just because it is publicly accessible.

## Tutor Teaching Rules

When a learner makes a mistake, the tutor should:
1. Correct the sentence.
2. Explain the relevant rule in simple English.
3. Explain the meaning and when the structure is used.
4. Provide two or three level-appropriate examples.
5. Ask the learner to practice.
6. Track recurring errors and revisit them later.

Teach grammar through useful examples and practice, not grammar tables alone.

## Original Exercise Policy

The tutor may create new exercises inspired by the skills, difficulty, and task structures of official model tests.

It should not claim AI-generated exercises are official questions. Copyrighted textbook or examination content must not be copied or redistributed without appropriate permission.
